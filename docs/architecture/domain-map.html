Key Decisions

  • ResaleRequest is a separate aggregate in Homeownership — right-of-first-refusal and resale price calculation happen before the PurchaseWorkflow starts, keeping the saga clean.
  • PurchaseWorkflow handles both initial purchase and resale — the same 5 parallel tracks (eligibility, financing, inspection, title, lease execution) apply to both. Resale just has a ResaleRequest pre-step.
  • ImprovementRequest lives in Homeownership (not Stewardship) because approval directly updates the resale price basis on GroundLease.
  • Stewardship is a separate BC — different language (case, compliance, concern vs. lease, purchase, closing) and different operational team (housing counselors). Could fold into Homeownership for small orgs.
  • LeaseFeeAccount is separate from GroundLease — financial operations (payment plans, late notices, waivers) have their own lifecycle independent of lease terms.
  • Property Registry is foundational — LandParcel and Building must exist before a GroundLease can be created. References are by ID only; Homeownership does not own those aggregates.

Open Questions

  • Should Stewardship fold into Homeownership, or is the team/language separation real enough to justify its own BC?
  • Does Membership warrant its own BC, or is a Member just a User with a role flag in an Identity context?
  • CLTFirstRefusalExercised path: when CLT buys back the home, does a new PurchaseWorkflow start with the CLT as buyer, or is that a separate aggregate/process?
  • LeaseFeeAccount — push model (scheduler emits MonthlyLeaseFeeDue) or pull model (homeowner portal queries overdue fees)? Impacts whether Finance BC needs an async scheduler.
  • RenovationStarted / RenovationCompleted on Building — should this be a separate RenovationProject aggregate in PropertyRegistry, or just status transitions on Building?
