Key Decisions

  • Two-stage applicant intake: InterestRegistration is a pre-application funnel — interest form, information session, housing counseling. Only after counseling completion does an Application open. CLTs universally require counseling before qualification; it belongs in the model.
  • NoticeOfIntentToSell replaces ResaleRequest: The CLT's right-of-first-refusal process starts the moment a homeowner signals intent. This aggregate owns the ROFR clock (model ground lease: 90 days), the price negotiation, and the waive/exercise decision — before a PurchaseWorkflow ever starts.
  • ROFRExercised path: When CLT exercises first refusal, a PurchaseWorkflow starts with CLT as buyer — same five-track saga, just CLT takes the buyer role. The home enters a brief CLT-owned period then a new sale to a waitlisted buyer. This is modelled as two sequential workflows, not a special case.
  • ImprovementRequest is a per-item collection, not a scalar: Each improvement (deck, HVAC, solar) has its own approval, actual cost, and depreciation schedule. The depreciated total is computed at resale time by summing approved items. A single decimal? ApprovedImprovements field would lose all of this.
  • MortgageConsent lives in Homeownership: When a homeowner refinances, the CLT must review the new loan terms against the ground lease's mortgage restrictions and issue written consent. Each consent is a discrete record — not a flag on GroundLease — because CLTs audit these for compliance with recognition agreements.
  • LenderRecognitionAgreement is a standing tri-party instrument: Executed once per lender per lease. It grants the CLT a 60-day cure period on mortgage default before foreclosure can proceed. It sits in Homeownership (not Finance) because it governs the lease relationship, not payment terms.
  • EstoppelCertificate is its own aggregate: Lenders demand this time-sensitive document (lease in good standing, no defaults, current fee payment) before any financing closes. It has a response deadline (typically 10 business days) and an audit trail — worth tracking as first-class objects.
  • ForeclosurePreventionCase lives in Stewardship: CLTs intervene early when mortgage payments are distressed — counseling referrals, forbearance negotiation, activating the 60-day cure right. This is stewardship work (housing counselors) not finance work (fee collection). Triggered by LeaseFeeArrearsEscalated from Lease Finance or by servicer delinquency reports received directly.
  • Stewardship is a separate BC: Different language (case, violation, cure vs. lease, purchase, closing), different operational team (housing counselors vs. finance staff), different cadence (annual visits vs. monthly fees). The overlap is in monitoring signals — those cross-BC events are explicitly modelled.
  • Property Registry is foundational: LandParcel and Building must exist before a GroundLease can reference them. Homeownership holds IDs only — no ownership of those aggregates.
  • LeaseFeeAccount is separate from GroundLease: Payment plans, late notices, hardship waivers, and arrears escalation have their own lifecycle independent of lease terms. Finance staff operate this BC; lease administrators operate Homeownership.

Open Questions

  • Index formula and improvements: In the index-based resale formula (AMI or CPI), improvements generally do not directly add to the resale price — the index adjustment already tracks value growth. Only the appraisal-based formula uses a market appraisal delta where CLT-approved improvements can affect the appraisal outcome. The itemized formula explicitly credits improvement costs. Needs verification against the ground lease template before implementing ResaleCalculationInput.
  • OriginalAppraisalValue vs OriginalPurchasePrice: The current ResaleCalculationInput conflates the subsidized purchase price (what the homeowner paid) with the original unencumbered market appraisal (the baseline for appraisal-based formula delta). These are two distinct values and must be stored separately.
  • Fractional ownership years in fixed-percentage formula: Current model uses integer years. Should be days / 365.25 to avoid rounding distortion on mid-year resales.
  • Should Membership warrant its own BC? A Member may just be a User with a membership role in an Identity context. CLTs have voting membership (annual dues, meeting attendance) — enough lifecycle to argue for its own BC, but small orgs may fold this into a role flag.
  • Servicer delinquency data feed: CLTs receive monthly mortgage payment reports from loan servicers. Does DistressSignalDetected come from a scheduled import job, a push from a servicer API, or manual entry? Impacts whether Finance BC needs an async scheduler or an inbound integration event.
  • RenovationProject aggregate in PropertyRegistry: Building has RenovationStarted / RenovationCompleted transitions. For major rehabs, CLTs track contractor, scope, permits, and inspection outcomes over months. Worth a dedicated RenovationProject aggregate rather than status transitions on Building?
  • CLT as buyer in ROFRExercised path: Two sequential PurchaseWorkflows (CLT buys, then CLT sells to waitlist buyer) implies a brief period where CLT appears as homeowner on the GroundLease. Does GroundLease support a CLT_OWNED phase between transfers, or is this modelled as lease termination + new lease creation?
