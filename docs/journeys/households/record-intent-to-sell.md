# Record Intent to Sell

For when a homeowner tells the trust, by phone, email, letter or in person, that they want to sell their home. Recording it opens a resale and starts the trust's purchase option window.

1. User navigates to Households and searches for the homeowner by member name or email (see [Find Household](find-household.md))
2. User clicks the row to open the household detail; a household that owns a home is at the Homeowner stage
3. The Next Step card names the home and says since when the household has owned it, with "Record Intent to Sell" and "Open Ground Lease"
4. User clicks "Record Intent to Sell"; a form opens in the card
5. User enters Notice Date (default today, not in the future), selects how the notice came in (Phone, Email, Letter or In Person), and optionally enters Notes
6. User clicks "Record Intent" (Cancel closes the form without recording anything)
7. Toast: "Intent to sell recorded"
8. The Next Step card changes to say the household has given notice to sell, and shows the Notice Date, how it came in, the Option Window End (the notice date plus the lease's option window) and the days left, or "Window passed" once that date is behind, with an "Open Resale" button. The lease's Resale section shows the resale too, without a button to record it
9. Shortly after:
    - The resale appears on Resales
    - The home shows Resale on Homes
