# View Household Detail

1. User opens a household from the Households table, or arrives via a link to it
2. Panel opens titled with the primary contact's name, with their email as subtitle
3. Panel shows the household's current Household Size, Registered date, Annual Income and Accessibility Need, with "Edit Circumstances"
4. "Household Members" lists every member with name, email and phone; the primary contact is labelled "Primary Contact". Each member has "Edit", every member but the primary contact has "Remove", and "Add Member" adds one
5. "Buyer Preparation Checklist" shows Housing Counseling, CLT Orientation and Mortgage Prequalification as done or pending (read-only here; record them from the application detail)
6. "Applications" lists every application the household has made, with phase and submitted date; "Open" opens it on the Applications page
7. When the household has an open (Submitted or Approved) application, "Open Active Application" opens it; otherwise "Start Application" is shown
