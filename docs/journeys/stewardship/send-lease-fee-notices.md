# Send Lease Fee Notices

For the trust's regular round of late notices: print every letter that is due, post them, and record them as sent.

1. User navigates to Stewardship, clicks the Lease Fees tab, and switches the filter to "Notice due" (or follows a Lease fee notice item on Today to its lease, see [Record Lease Fee Notice](../ground-leases/record-lease-fee-notice.md))
2. The table lists each active lease whose oldest unpaid charge has passed the trust's notice days (see [Set Lease Fee Notice Policy](../trust-policy/set-lease-fee-notice-policy.md)) and that has not had that notice since its arrears began. The Notice column shows "First due" or "Second due"
3. User checks the list: a household that has paid but whose payment isn't recorded yet is fixed by recording the payment first (see [Record Lease Fee Payment](../ground-leases/record-lease-fee-payment.md)), which takes it off the list
4. User clicks "Print Notices". A print page opens in a new tab with one letter per lease, a page each, and the browser's print dialog
5. Each letter has today's date, the homeowner's name, the home, the heading "Lease fee past due" (first) or "Second notice: lease fee past due" (second), the amount due, the months unpaid and the monthly fee, a request to pay or get in touch, and the trust's sign-off. The second notice also says that continued non-payment can lead to a formal notice of default under the ground lease. The letter names no lease section, since trusts number their leases differently
6. User posts or hands over the letters, then clicks "Record Notices Sent"
7. "Record Notices Sent" panel opens with every lease with a notice due, each checked, showing homeowner, notice level and amount. User unticks any not sent, and sets Date Sent (default today) and Delivery (First-Class Mail, Certified Mail, Registered Mail, Hand Delivered or Email; default First-Class Mail)
8. User clicks "Record N Notices", or "Cancel"
9. Toast: "N notices recorded". The leases leave the "Notice due" list, and their Notice column shows the notice sent and its date
10. If a notice can't be recorded (for example the lease was paid up in the meantime), the panel stays open and lists which leases failed and why; the rest are recorded
