# Resolve Foreclosure Prevention Case

1. User navigates to Stewardship, Foreclosure Prevention tab, and opens an Active case's detail panel (a case becomes Active once a referral, forbearance request, or cure period is recorded; until then the Resolution section instead says to record one first)
2. At the bottom of the panel, in the Resolution section, selects Resolution Outcome (Intervention Successful, Homeowner Sold, Cure Period Exercised, or Lease Terminated; defaults to Intervention Successful) and confirms Resolution Date (defaulted to today)
3. User clicks "Resolve Case"
4. Toast: "Case resolved"
5. Panel subtitle shows Resolved, the outcome and resolved date appear at the top, and the action forms no longer appear
6. Case leaves the Foreclosure Prevention table, which lists only unresolved cases
