# Invite Staff

For when a trust adds a colleague to CommonGround.

1. User clicks **Team** in the sidebar, under Settings
2. The Members table lists everyone in the trust (Name, Email). The Pending Invites table lists invites not yet used (Email, Invited By, Expires); it is hidden when there are none
3. User clicks "Invite Staff", enters the colleague's Email and clicks "Create Invite Link". Email is required and must look like an address
4. The panel shows the invite link with a "Copy Link" button, and says the link works once and expires in 7 days. The link is shown only this once; the user copies it into their own email to the colleague
5. The invite appears in Pending Invites
6. To withdraw an invite, user clicks "Cancel" on its row and confirms. The link stops working and the row is removed
7. When the colleague joins, they appear in Members and the invite leaves Pending Invites
