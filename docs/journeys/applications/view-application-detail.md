# View Application Detail

1. User navigates to Applications and clicks a row in the Applications table, or clicks an "Application to decide" or "Qualification to confirm" row on [Today](../today/work-through-today.md)
2. Detail panel opens titled with the household's primary contact, showing phase (Submitted, Approved, Selected, Denied, Withdrawn, or Removed), submitted at/by, primary contact phone, household size, annual income and accessibility need. These follow the household while the application is Submitted or Approved; a Selected application keeps the figures it was chosen on. Phase-specific fields appear only when they apply: approved date when Approved or Selected, selected date when Selected, denial reason when Denied, withdrawal reason when Withdrawn, removed date, reason and note when Removed, and Qualification Confirmed (date and who) once staff have confirmed the household still qualifies
3. When an Approved application's income or size changed after approval, a warning at the top says "Qualification to confirm since {date}" and what changed, and that the household can't be chosen until staff confirm it still qualifies
4. "Open Household" (styled as a link, as are the other connected-record buttons) opens the household on the Households page, where its members and readiness (counseling, orientation, prequalification) are kept
5. If the applicant has been selected, the panel shows "Open Selected Land Parcel" and "Open Selected Home" links
6. When the household has been passed over, a Passed Over section lists each time another household was chosen for a home while this one could have been: the date, the home, the selection method (Priority or Lottery), the reason given, and an "Open Selection" button that opens the home's selection page. It covers the household's earlier applications too. With no pass-overs the section is not shown.
7. A Workflow Actions section shows the actions available for the current phase: Submitted offers Approve Application, Deny Application, and Withdraw Application; Approved offers Remove from Waiting List and Withdraw Application, and Confirm Still Qualifies while the application is marked; Approve Application and Confirm Still Qualifies are primary buttons; Deny Application, Remove from Waiting List and Withdraw Application sit in a separate group below it, divided by a line, in red text; otherwise "No actions available for this phase" — choosing a household for a home is done from the [home's selection page](../home-selections/view-home-selection.md), not from the application
8. User closes the panel
