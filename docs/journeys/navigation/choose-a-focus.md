# Choose a Focus

1. The sidebar groups pages by the work: **Work** in process order (Households, Applications, Waiting list, Purchases, Stewardship, Resales), **Records** (Land parcels, Development, Homes, Ground leases, Documents) and **Settings** (Lease templates)
2. User picks a focus from "My focus" at the bottom of the sidebar: Everything (default), Intake, Sales or Stewardship
   - Intake covers Households, Applications and Waiting list
   - Sales covers Purchases and Resales
   - Stewardship covers Stewardship
3. With Everything, Work lists every page. With any other focus, Work splits into **My work** (the pages in the focus) and a collapsed **Other work** toggle; clicking the toggle shows or hides the rest. Records and Settings don't change
4. The focus and whether Other work is open are remembered in this browser; if they can't be read, the focus is Everything
5. Opening the app at its root lands on the first page under My work (Households for Everything and Intake, Purchases for Sales, Stewardship for Stewardship)

Focus only orders the sidebar; every page stays reachable.
