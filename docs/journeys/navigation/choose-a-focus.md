# Choose a Focus

1. **Today** and **Reminders** sit at the top of the sidebar, outside any group. Below them the sidebar groups pages by the work: **Work** in process order (Households, Applications, Waiting list, Home selections, Purchases, Stewardship, Resales), **Records** (Land parcels, Development, Homes, Ground leases, Documents) and **Settings** (Lease templates, Trust policy)
2. User picks a focus from "My focus" at the bottom of the sidebar: Everything (default), Intake, Sales or Stewardship
   - Intake covers Households, Applications and Waiting list
   - Sales covers Home selections, Purchases and Resales
   - Stewardship covers Stewardship
3. With Everything, Work lists every page. With any other focus, Work splits into **My work** (the pages in the focus) and a collapsed **Other work** toggle; clicking the toggle shows or hides the rest. Records and Settings don't change
4. Sidebar counts match the pages they lead to ([Today](../today/work-through-today.md) step 10):
   - Today's count is what Today shows under My areas: with a focus it counts only that focus's areas plus Records and every reminder, with Everything it counts every item
   - The collapsed **Other work** toggle shows the sum of its pages' counts; expanded, each page shows its own
5. The focus and whether Other work is open are remembered in this browser; if they can't be read, the focus is Everything
6. Opening the app at its root lands on [Today](../today/work-through-today.md), which shows the focus's areas
7. The small label above each page's title is the name of its sidebar group (Work, Records or Settings), so Ground leases says "Records" and Households says "Work". Today shows the date there, and a single home selection keeps "Home Selection"

Focus only orders the sidebar; every page stays reachable.
