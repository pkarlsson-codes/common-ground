# Start Home Selection

1. User navigates to Homes and opens the detail panel of a home at the Available stage, or at the Resale stage once the trust has declined to buy it
2. The Next Step card reads "Choose a household from the waiting list for this home.", followed by how many earlier selections were cancelled (if any)
3. User clicks "Start Selection" on the card
4. The selection page opens at Homes › Home Selection with every household on the [Waiting List](../waiting-list/view-waiting-list.md) as a candidate, their priority facts snapshotted as they were when the selection started. Each candidate's income, as declared on the application, is judged against the home's income limit for its household size, using the [income limits](../income-limits/enter-income-limits.md) in effect today. The selection keeps the limits it judged by

If the waiting list is empty, the card shows "The waiting list is empty, so there is no one to select from." and no selection is started.

If the home has an income limit and no income limits in effect today have that level, the start is rejected with "No income limits for 80% of AMI are in effect on Nov 12, 2026. Enter them under Income Limits." A home with no income limit starts without the check.

A home has at most one open or completed selection. If someone else started one first, the start is rejected and the card shows "This home already has an open or completed selection." Once a selection is cancelled, or the purchase it led to fails or is withdrawn, a new one can be started.

When a purchase that came from a selection fails or is withdrawn, a new selection for the home is started automatically, so the home goes straight back to the Selection stage. It is not started when nobody is waiting, or when the home has an income limit and no income limits in effect today have that level; staff start it once they enter them.
