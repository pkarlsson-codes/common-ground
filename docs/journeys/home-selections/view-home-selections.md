# View Home Selections

1. User navigates to Home selections (under Work, and under My work for the Sales focus)
2. Table lists the households chosen for homes across every home, newest first: Home (its label), Chosen Household, Method (Priority or Lottery), On the Waiting List (how many households the choice was made from), and Chosen On
3. User clicks a row to open that home's selection page

A home selection is one decision and is never reopened. Homes waiting for a household to be chosen are on [Today](../today/work-through-today.md) and the Homes page, not here.
