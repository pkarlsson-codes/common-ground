# Set Lease Fee Notice Policy

For when a trust sets how soon it follows up on unpaid lease fees, and what its letters are signed with.

1. User clicks **Trust policy** in the sidebar, under Settings
2. The Lease Fee Notices section shows the current policy: First Notice After (days overdue, default 10), Second Notice After (days overdue, default 30) and Letter Sign-Off (the lines printed at the end of every notice letter, such as the trust's name, address and phone; empty by default). A trust that has never saved the policy sees the defaults, with "Using the default policy"
3. User changes the values and clicks "Save Policy". First Notice After must be at least 1; Second Notice After must be more than First Notice After
4. Toast: "Policy saved". The values stay on the page, and the default note is gone
5. From then on, the Lease Fees tab, the lease panel and Today judge notices by the new days, and new letters carry the new sign-off. Notices already recorded keep what they were
