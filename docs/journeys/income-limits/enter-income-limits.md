# Enter Income Limits

For when a trust records the income limits its homes are bound by, usually once a year when HUD or the state agency publishes new ones.

1. User clicks **Income Limits** in the sidebar, under Settings
2. The page lists the trust's schedules, newest Effective From first: Effective From, the levels it holds (e.g. "50%, 80%, 120% of AMI"), and who entered it. The schedule in effect today is marked "In effect". With no schedules the page says "No income limits entered. Selections for homes with an income limit can't start until you enter them."
3. User clicks "Enter Income Limits"
4. "Enter Income Limits" panel opens with Effective From (defaults to today) and one row per level. The rows start with the levels of the newest schedule and empty amounts, or one empty row if there is none
5. Each row has Percent of AMI and the limit for 1 to 8 people, as published. User fills in the amounts, adds levels with "Add Level" and removes them with "Remove"
6. User clicks "Save Income Limits". Every row needs a percent from 1 to 200 and all eight amounts above zero; no two rows can have the same percent; the effective date can't be one another schedule already has
7. Toast: "Income limits saved". The panel closes and the new schedule appears in the list

A household of more than 8 people gets the 8-person limit plus 8% of the 4-person limit for each person over 8, rounded to the nearest $50, as HUD does for sizes its tables do not print.

To correct a schedule, user clicks it in the list, then "Correct". The same panel opens filled in, and "Save Income Limits" replaces it. Selections already started keep the limits they were judged by.
