# Enter Income Limits

For when a trust records the income limits its homes are bound by, usually once a year when HUD or the state agency publishes new ones.

1. User clicks **Income Limits** in the sidebar, under Settings, or **Enter limits** on a Today item saying limits are missing or out of date (see [Work Through Today](../today/work-through-today.md)), which opens the page with the panel of step 4 already open
2. The page lists the trust's schedules, newest Effective From first: Effective From, the levels it holds (e.g. "50%, 80%, 120% of AMI"), and who entered it. The schedule in effect today is marked "In effect". With no schedules the page says "No income limits entered. Selections for homes with an income limit can't start until you enter them."
3. User clicks "Enter Income Limits"
4. "Enter Income Limits" panel opens with Effective From (defaults to today) and a table laid out like HUD's: one row per level, a % of AMI column, then one column per household size 1 to 8. The rows start with the levels of the newest schedule and empty amounts, or one empty row if there is none
5. User fills in the amounts, adds levels with "Add Level" and removes them with "Remove". Amounts may be typed with $ signs and commas. Pasting a block copied from HUD's spreadsheet, web page or PDF fills the cells right of and below the cell pasted into, and adds levels when the block has more rows than the table. Words in the block are dropped, a number with a % sign (as in "Very Low (50%) Income Limits") sets that row's percent of AMI, a "1 2 3 … 8" household size header is skipped, and when a row has more numbers than cells to fill, the last ones are used (HUD rows can start with the median income)
6. User clicks "Save Income Limits". Every row needs a percent from 1 to 200 and all eight amounts above zero; no two rows can have the same percent; the effective date can't be one another schedule already has
7. Toast: "Income limits saved". The panel closes and the new schedule appears in the list

A household of more than 8 people gets the 8-person limit plus 8% of the 4-person limit for each person over 8, rounded to the nearest $50, as HUD does for sizes its tables do not print.

To correct a schedule, user clicks it in the list, which shows its limits in the same layout, then "Correct". The same panel opens filled in, and "Save Income Limits" replaces it. Selections already started keep the limits they were judged by.
