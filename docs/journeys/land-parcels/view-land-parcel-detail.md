# View Land Parcel Detail

1. User navigates to Land Parcels, optionally searching by parcel number or address to narrow the table
2. User clicks a row in the Land Parcels table
3. Detail panel opens showing status (Active or Decommissioned), area (acres), acquisition method, acquisition date, registered by, covenant terms (if any), and registered at
4. Panel also shows a Homes section listing homes on the parcel with their label, type, bedrooms/bathrooms, and status; clicking one opens it
5. Panel also shows a Development Projects section listing projects on the parcel with their name and status
6. A Connected Records section links to the ground leases on the parcel's homes, each with its homeowner and status
7. User can collapse or expand the Homes and Development Projects sections independently
8. User closes the panel
