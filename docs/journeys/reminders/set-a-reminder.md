# Set a Reminder

A reminder is a dated note for work that has no record behind it, such as "call the lender Thursday". It belongs to the trust: every member sees it, whatever their focus.

1. User clicks **New reminder**: in the Today header, in a Today section's header, or on the Reminders page
2. The New reminder panel opens with:
   - **Date**: the day it starts showing on Today. Defaults to today; a day before today is refused
   - **Section**: the Today section it shows in. A section header's button picks that section; the Today header and the Reminders page pick None, which puts it in the Reminders section
   - **Note**: what to do, at most 500 characters
3. On **Set reminder**, the panel closes and the toast says "Reminder set"
4. A reminder for today appears on Today straight away, first in its section. A later one is listed under Upcoming on the [Reminders page](view-reminders.md) until its day comes
