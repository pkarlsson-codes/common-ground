# Record Payoff Statement

1. User opens an open resale (see [View Resale Detail](view-resale-detail.md)) and clicks "Payoff Statement"
2. "Payoff Statement" panel opens with Good Through (default today, from today to 60 days ahead)
3. Panel shows Lease Fees Owed, Interest Owed, Credit and Total Owed as of Good Through, and the Oldest Unpaid Month. A zero total says "Nothing owed"
4. Changing Good Through recalculates the figures
5. User clicks "Print Letter", or "Close"
6. The letter opens in a print view, addressed to the closing agent, naming the homeowner, the home, Good Through and Total Owed
7. Toast: "Payoff statement recorded"
8. The resale's Payoff Statements list gains a row: date printed, user, Good Through, Total Owed. Newest first
