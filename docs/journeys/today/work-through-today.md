# Work Through Today

1. User opens the app at its root, or clicks **Today** at the top of the sidebar
2. The header shows today's date in the trust's time zone and how many things need attention
3. Items are grouped into sections in work order: Applicants, Selections, Purchases, Stewardship, Records. A section with nothing waiting is hidden
4. Within a section, each kind of item has a fixed place: Applicants: application to decide, readiness incomplete, prequalification expiring. Selections: home available, income limits missing, income limits out of date. Purchases: closing coming up, purchase stalled, purchase waiting. Stewardship: resale decision due, lease default not cured, mortgage request to decide, improvement request to decide, insurance short of the lease, insurance lapsed, lease fee notice due, resale closing to record, mortgage loan closing to confirm, resale price statement due, insurance ending soon. Records: document expiring. Items of one kind run due soonest first, then waiting longest. Each row shows what's waiting, the record (household, home or document), one line of detail and a due chip: red when overdue, amber when due within a week, plain otherwise. A row with no due date shows how long it has waited
5. What appears:
   - **Applicants**: applications waiting for a decision; households with an open application that are missing readiness steps; prequalifications expiring within 30 days
   - **Selections**: homes offered to the waiting list, waiting for a household to be chosen: "Available" for a home never sold, or available again after a purchase fell through, after the trust bought it back, or after the trust released it, waiting since then. Income limits: when a home has an income limit and the limits in effect today have no level for its percent (or no limits are in effect), one item "Income limits missing" whose detail names the missing percents and the homes households can't be chosen for (up to 3, then "and N more"). When the limits in effect are more than 12 months old and no later schedule is entered, one item "Income limits out of date", waiting since they turned 12 months old, since HUD publishes new limits every year. Neither appears when no home has an income limit
   - **Purchases**: every open purchase: stalled (due by its resolution target), closing within 14 days, or waiting on the steps it hasn't recorded
   - **Stewardship**: active leases with a late fee notice or notice of default due (see [Send Lease Fee Notices](../stewardship/send-lease-fee-notices.md)); the detail gives the amount due and days overdue. Also active leases whose notice of default wasn't cured by its deadline, due 30 days after it, when the trust says whether it exercises its purchase option (Art 12.4(b)); the detail gives the deadline, what is still to pay of the amount owed and the due date. The item goes once the amount is paid or the lease ends, and is left out while the lease has an unresolved foreclosure prevention case (it comes back if the case is resolved and the default is still not cured). Also mortgage requests waiting for a decision, due on their expected closing date; the detail gives the purpose, lender and principal and the business days to closing. And approved mortgage loans whose expected closing date has passed with no closing recorded, due the day after it; they go once the loan is recorded closed or the request is withdrawn. And active leases with no resale in progress whose yearly resale price statement is due, due a year after the last statement (or the lease start when none was sent); the item goes once a statement is recorded (see [Send Resale Price Statements](../stewardship/send-resale-price-statements.md)). Also active leases whose insurance cover has ended or has no certificate on file ("Insurance lapsed"), or ends today or within 30 days ("Insurance ending soon"), due on the policy end date (or the lease start with none); the detail says "Policy ends {date}", "Policy ended {date}" or "No certificate on file". And active leases whose current insurance certificate falls short of the lease, waiting since the day it was received, with no due date; the detail names each shortfall. And open resales (see [View Open Resales](../resales/view-open-resales.md)): one item per resale where the trust's purchase option is still undecided (notice given or appraised, no decision), waiting since the notice date and due on the option window end (Art 10.5, 10.6); it stays, overdue, after the window ends until staff decide or withdraw the resale. And one item per resale whose option was exercised and whose buyback closing isn't recorded, waiting since the option was exercised, with no due date. A withdrawn resale and a closed resale have no item
   - **Records**: documents expiring within 30 days, or expired
6. Each row offers the actions that fix it, one button per fix:
   - **Application to decide**: Approve, Deny
   - **Readiness incomplete**: Record housing counseling, Record CLT orientation, Record prequalification (only the missing steps)
   - **Prequalification expiring**: Record prequalification
   - **Home available**: Choose household, which opens the home's selection page
   - **Income limits missing** or **out of date**: Enter limits, which opens the Income Limits page with the Enter Income Limits panel open; clicking the row opens the page
   - **Purchase waiting or stalled**: one button per step not yet recorded (Record eligibility, appraisal, financing, inspection, title, lease signing), or Schedule closing once every step is in; **Closing coming up**: Record closing
   - **Lease fee notice due**: Record notice, which opens the lease, where the letter is printed and the notice recorded
   - **Lease default not cured**: Review default, which opens the lease. Ending the lease (Art 12.4) or working with the homeowner is staff's decision; nothing in the app ends it automatically. To work with the homeowner, staff open a foreclosure prevention case from the lease (see [Open Foreclosure Prevention Case from a Lease Default](../ground-leases/open-foreclosure-prevention-case-from-default.md))
   - **Mortgage request to decide**: Decide, or Add appraisal while the request has no appraisal; both open the request (see [View Mortgage Request](../ground-leases/view-mortgage-request.md))
   - **Improvement request to decide**: Decide, which opens the request (see [View Improvement Request](../ground-leases/view-improvement-request.md)). Due two weeks after it was received; red once passed
   - **Mortgage loan closing to confirm**: Confirm closing, which opens Record Loan Closed (see [Record Mortgage Loan Closed](../ground-leases/record-mortgage-loan-closed.md)); clicking the row opens the lease
   - **Resale price statement due**: Prepare statement, which opens the Resale Price Statement panel over Today (see [Prepare Resale Price Statement](../ground-leases/prepare-resale-price-statement.md)); clicking the row opens the lease
   - **Resale decision due**: Record appraisal while the resale has none, and Buy the home (see [Exercise Purchase Option](../resales/exercise-purchase-option.md)); each opens its panel over Today; clicking the row opens the resale
   - **Resale closing to record**: Record buyback closing, which opens its panel over Today (see [Record Buyback Closed](../resales/record-buyback-closed.md)); clicking the row opens the resale
   - **Insurance lapsed** or **Insurance ending soon**: Record certificate, which opens the Record Insurance Certificate panel over Today (see [Record Insurance Certificate](../ground-leases/record-insurance-certificate.md)); clicking the row opens the lease
   - **Insurance short of the lease**: Record certificate, which opens the Record Insurance Certificate panel over Today; clicking the row opens the lease, where staff can also change the requirements
   - **Document expiring**: none; the row itself opens the document
7. An action opens its panel over Today; on success Today refreshes and the item leaves or changes. The purchase steps open the record's detail for now, where the action is
8. Clicking anywhere else on a row opens the record's detail
9. With a focus other than Everything, only the focus's areas and Records are shown, with a note saying how many items are hidden; the **My areas / All areas** toggle shows the rest.
10. Sidebar items show how many items are waiting on their page. The Today item shows the count the page shows under My areas (every item with Everything). See [Choose a Focus](../navigation/choose-a-focus.md)
11. An item leaves Today once the record no longer needs attention (for example, an application is approved or denied)
