# Record Intent to Sell

For when a homeowner tells the trust, by phone, email, letter or in person, that they want to sell their home. Recording it opens a resale and starts the trust's purchase option window.

1. User navigates to Ground Leases
2. User optionally searches by homeowner name or email, then clicks an Active lease to open the detail panel
3. The Resale section says "No resale in progress" and shows a "Record Intent to Sell" button (an Inactive lease shows neither)
4. User clicks "Record Intent to Sell"; a form opens in the section
5. User enters Notice Date (default today, not in the future), selects how the notice came in (Phone, Email, Letter or In Person), and optionally enters Notes
6. User clicks "Record Intent" (Cancel closes the form without recording anything)
7. Toast: "Intent to sell recorded"
8. The Resale section shows the Notice Date, how it came in, the notes, the Option Window End (the notice date plus the lease's option window) and the days left, or "Window passed" once that date is behind
9. Shortly after:
    - The resale appears on Resales
    - The home shows Resale on Homes
