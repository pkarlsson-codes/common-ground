# View Heir Transfer

1. User clicks a transfer in a lease's Heir Transfer section (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md))
2. "Heir Transfer" panel opens, titled with the home; its subtitle is the deceased homeowner's name and the status: Open, Heir recorded, Completed or Closed
3. Panel shows Date of Death, Notice Received On with "Notice was late" when it was more than 90 days after the death, Notified By and Contact, and the notes
4. Once an heir is recorded: the heir's name, email and phone, the relationship (Spouse, Child, Household member since a date, Other), and for Other the verified income, household size, the home's limit and the income documents with download links
5. Open or Heir recorded: "Record Heir" (or "Change Heir" once one is recorded) and "Close Without Transfer"; Heir recorded also shows "Complete Transfer"
6. Once completed: Transfer Date, the Letter of Agreement and Attorney's Acknowledgment with download links, the notes and "Print Letter"
7. Once closed: Closed On, the reason and the notes
