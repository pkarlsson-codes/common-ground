# View Improvement Request

1. User clicks a request in a lease's Improvements section (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)), or Decide on an "Improvement request to decide" item on Today
2. "Improvement Request" panel opens, titled with the description; its subtitle is the homeowner's name and the status: Waiting for decision, Approved, Denied, Completed or Withdrawn
3. Panel shows Received On with the reply due date (Received On plus 14 days; red with "Reply overdue by N days" when passed and the request is still waiting), Estimated Cost, and the plans files with download links
4. While waiting for a decision: "Approve" and "Deny" buttons, and "Withdraw" for when the homeowner drops the work
5. Once approved: Approved On, the Pre-Approved Credit, the lease's improvements cap and the credit the lease already had when approved (they don't change if the lease's terms do), the notes, "Print Letter", and "Record Completed" and "Withdraw"
6. Once denied: Denied On, the reason and "Print Letter"
7. Once completed: Completed On, Actual Cost, the receipts with download links, the Confirmed Credit (what counts toward the resale price) and the notes
8. Once withdrawn: the date and the reason
