# Close Heir Transfer

For when the home does not pass to the heir: the heir is not income-qualified, declines, or another reason. The lease is unchanged. The home is then sold under the resale terms (see [Record Intent to Sell](../households/record-intent-to-sell.md)).

1. In the Heir Transfer panel of an Open or Heir recorded transfer (see [View Heir Transfer](view-heir-transfer.md)), user clicks "Close Without Transfer"
2. "Close Heir Transfer" panel opens
3. User chooses a Reason (Heir not qualified, Heir declined, Other) and optionally adds Notes
4. User clicks "Close Transfer", or "Cancel"
5. Toast: "Transfer closed". The transfer shows Closed with the reason, and "Open Heir Transfer" is available again on the lease
