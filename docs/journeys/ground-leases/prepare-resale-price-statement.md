# Prepare Resale Price Statement

For one homeowner: print a letter with the home's current maximum resale price under the lease's formula (Ch 23, Disclosure), and record it sent. Used for a homeowner who asks, and for a statement due on Today. For the yearly round of every lease due, see [Send Resale Price Statements](../stewardship/send-resale-price-statements.md).

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)) and clicks "Prepare Statement" in its Resale Price Statement section, or clicks "Prepare statement" on a Resale price statement due item on Today, which opens the panel over Today
2. "Resale Price Statement" panel opens, showing the homeowner, the home, the formula and the Base Price. It has Statement Date (default today; not in the future, not before the lease start). The date is the letter's date, the date the price is worked out on, and the date recorded as sent
3. The panel asks only for what the formula needs:
   - Appraisal-based: Market Value and Value Source ("County assessment 2026"), both required. The panel says "An estimate of what the home would sell for today, not an appraisal." When the lease has no Original Appraised Value, the panel says "This lease has no original appraised value, so its resale price can't be worked out." and has no Print or Record buttons
   - Index-based: "{Index type} value on the statement date", required, with the baseline value shown
   - Fixed-rate: nothing
   - When the lease has an improvements cap: Improvements Credit (optional; defaults to the lease's Confirmed Improvements), with "Approved improvements, up to $X"
4. Once the figures are in, the panel shows the Maximum Resale Price and the working, one line per figure, as on the letter
5. User clicks "Print Letter". A print page opens in a new tab with the letter and the browser's print dialog. The letter has the statement date, the homeowner's name, the home, the heading "Your home's resale price", the sentence "Under your ground lease, the most your home can sell for on {date} is $X.", the working (for example "Purchase price $180,000.00 × CPI-U on {date} 312.5 ÷ CPI-U at purchase 290.0 = $193,965.52"; "Purchase price $180,000.00 plus 25% of the rise in market value: market value $320,000.00 (County assessment 2026) less $260,000.00 at purchase = $195,000.00"; "Purchase price $180,000.00 plus 2% a year for 6 full years = $202,709.24"; "Approved improvements credit: $4,000.00"), then: "If you decide to sell, the trust may buy your home at this price or at its appraised value then, whichever is lower, worked out when you give notice. The price will change with the formula until then. This letter is for your information and is not an offer." and the trust's sign-off
6. User posts, emails or hands over the letter, then sets Delivery (First-Class Mail, Certified Mail, Registered Mail, Hand Delivered or Email; default First-Class Mail) and clicks "Record Sent", or "Cancel"
7. Toast: "Statement recorded". The panel closes. The lease's Resale Price Statement section lists the statement and the next one is due a year after the statement date. A Resale price statement due item for the lease leaves Today
