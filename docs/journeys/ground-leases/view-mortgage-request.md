# View Mortgage Request

1. User clicks a request under Requests in a lease's Mortgages section (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)), or Decide or Add appraisal on a "Mortgage request to decide" item on Today
2. "Mortgage Request" panel opens, titled with the purpose and lender ("Refinance with First Federal"); its subtitle is the homeowner's name and the status: Waiting for decision, Approved, Denied, Closed or Withdrawn
3. Panel shows Received On, Expected Closing with the business days between them (amber when fewer than 15), Principal, Total Debt After Closing, Interest Rate with Rate Type, Term, Closing Costs ("—" if none), and the appraisal ("$250,000.00 on March 3, 2027", or "Not received")
4. Panel shows a Debt Check:
   - With an appraisal: Purchase Option Price today (the lease's resale formula on the appraised value, and never above it), the lease's cap ("90% of the purchase option price: $X"), and "Total debt after closing is Y% of the purchase option price.", with "Within the cap." in green or "Above the cap by $Z." in red. On an index-based lease, the price also uses the current index value entered with the appraisal
   - Without an appraisal: "The debt check needs the loan's appraisal." with an "Add Appraisal" button (while waiting for a decision), which asks Appraised Value, Appraisal Date, on an index-based lease Current Index Value, and Closing Costs (optional). Toast: "Appraisal added"
5. While waiting for a decision: "Approve" and "Deny" buttons, and "Withdraw" for when the homeowner drops the loan
6. Once decided: the decision, its date, the reason when there is one, the figures the approval was made on (they don't change if the lease's terms do), and "Print Letter" (not on a withdrawn request)
7. Once approved: "Record Loan Closed" and "Withdraw". Once closed: the closing date and the mortgages it paid off
