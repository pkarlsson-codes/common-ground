# Edit Permitted Mortgage

For when the loan's servicing moves to another company, or staff get the address where notices go. Only the lender and the notice address change; the principal and dates stay as recorded.

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)) and clicks "Edit" on a mortgage in the Mortgages section (not shown on a paid-off mortgage)
2. "Edit Permitted Mortgage" panel opens with Lender or Servicer (required) and Notice Address (optional) filled in
3. User changes them and clicks "Save", or "Cancel"
4. Toast: "Mortgage updated". The section shows the new lender and address. Notices printed from now on are copied to the new address
