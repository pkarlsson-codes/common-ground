# Mark Mortgage Paid Off

For a mortgage paid off without a refinance the trust permitted: the term ended, the homeowner paid it early. A refinance marks the loans it paid off when it is recorded closed (see [Record Mortgage Loan Closed](record-mortgage-loan-closed.md)).

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)) and clicks "Paid Off" on a mortgage in the Mortgages section
2. "Mark Mortgage Paid Off" panel opens; its subtitle is the lender and the original principal
3. User enters Paid Off On (required, not in the future, not before the mortgage was permitted)
4. User clicks "Mark Paid Off", or "Cancel"
5. Toast: "Mortgage marked paid off". The mortgage moves below the current ones, greyed, with "Paid off {date}". It no longer counts toward debt on new requests and gets no copies of notices
