# Withdraw Intent to Sell

For when a homeowner who gave notice decides not to sell after all. Available until the trust exercises its purchase option or a household is selected as the buyer.

1. User opens a resale from Resales, or from "Open Resale" in a lease's Resale section (see [View Resale Detail](../resales/view-resale-detail.md))
2. In the panel's actions, user clicks "Withdraw"; a form opens in the panel
3. User enters Withdrawn Date (default today, not before the notice date or in the future) and a Reason (required)
4. User clicks "Withdraw Intent" (Cancel closes the form without withdrawing)
5. Toast: "Intent to sell withdrawn"
6. The resale closes as Withdrawn. The lease's Resale section says "No resale in progress". The household's Next Step card offers "Record Intent to Sell" again
7. Shortly after:
    - The resale is gone from Resales
    - The home shows Leased again on Homes

An appraisal already recorded stays on the withdrawn resale. Once the trust has exercised the option, or a household has been selected as the buyer, "Withdraw" is not offered. A selection still open when the homeowner withdraws is cancelled.
