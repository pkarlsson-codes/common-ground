# Withdraw Intent to Sell

For when a homeowner who gave notice decides not to sell after all.

1. User opens a lease with a resale in progress, from Ground Leases or from a row on Resales
2. In the Resale section, user clicks "Withdraw"; a form opens in the section
3. User enters Withdrawn Date (default today, not before the notice date or in the future) and a Reason (required)
4. User clicks "Withdraw Intent" (Cancel closes the form without withdrawing)
5. Toast: "Intent to sell withdrawn"
6. The Resale section says "No resale in progress" and shows "Record Intent to Sell" again
7. Shortly after:
    - The resale is gone from Resales
    - The home shows Leased again on Homes
