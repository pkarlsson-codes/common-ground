# Waive Lease Fee Interest

For late-fee interest the trust chooses not to collect: a household in hardship (Model Lease Art 5.4), a payment the bank delayed, or a settlement. Art 5.7 lets the trust require interest; it doesn't have to.

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)). The "Waive Interest" button shows in the Lease Fees section only while interest is owed
2. User clicks "Waive Interest". The "Waive Interest" panel opens with the homeowner and the interest owed today as subtitle, and says the waived amount comes off the balance while interest on fees that stay unpaid keeps adding up
3. Amount ($) starts at all the interest owed. User may lower it. Above the interest owed, the field says "Can't be more than $X." and the button is disabled
4. User enters a Reason (required)
5. User clicks "Waive Interest" (disabled until the amount is above zero and no more than is owed, and a reason is entered), or "Cancel"
6. A confirmation asks "Waive $X of interest? This can't be undone." User clicks "Waive Interest" to go ahead, or "Cancel" to return to the panel
7. Toast: "Interest waived"
8. The balance drops by the amount. The waiver shows under "Interest Waived" (Waived, Amount, Reason), newest first
