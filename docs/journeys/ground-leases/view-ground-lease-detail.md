# View Ground Lease Detail

1. User navigates to Ground Leases
2. User optionally searches by homeowner name or email (case-insensitive) and filters by status (All Statuses, Active, Inactive); the table lists newest leases first
3. User clicks a row in the Ground Leases table
4. Detail panel opens titled with the homeowner's name (the buyer, for a lease created from a purchase closing; the homeowner entered, for one created from Create Lease), showing status (Active or Inactive), resale formula, and term (years)
5. Depending on the formula, the panel shows Appreciation Share, Index Type, Baseline Index, and/or Annual %
6. Panel shows Base Price and Original Appraised Value (the figures the resale formula starts from; "—" if the lease has none), Improvements Cap if one is set, Start Date, Term, Option Window (days), homeowner Email and Phone (if present), and Covenant Terms (if present)
7. Panel shows Recorded On (the day the lease was recorded in CommonGround, which for a lease that predates CommonGround is later than its Start Date), and a Connected Records section linking to the home (by its label), its land parcel, and, for leases created from a purchase closing, the purchase and the household
8. Panel shows a Resale section: for an Active lease, the resale in progress (notice date, how it came in, Option Window End and days left, or "Window passed", its step, and an "Open Resale" button) or "No resale in progress". For an Inactive lease that ended in a buyback, the section links to the closed resale
9. Panel shows a Resale Price Statement section: Next Due (a year after the last statement, or after the lease start when none was sent; red when passed; "Not due while a resale is in progress" during a resale; absent on an Inactive lease), a "Prepare Statement" button (Active leases only), and the statements sent, newest first (Date, Price, Delivery, and the figures used, e.g. "Market value $320,000.00, County assessment 2026"). With none: "No statements sent"
10. Panel shows a Lease Fees section: Monthly Fee with its Land Use Fee and Repair Reserve Fee split, Late Interest ("10% a year, waived if paid within 30 days", or "None"), Fees Tracked From when tracking began after the lease start ("November 1, 2026, $150.00 owed then"; the amount only when one was owed), Balance as of today ("$120.00 due", in red, "$40.00 credit" or "Paid up"; with "includes $X interest" when interest is owed), Oldest Unpaid month and Days Overdue when there is a balance due, a "Record Payment" button, and the payment history newest first (Received, Amount, Method, Reference, and a Void action; a voided payment is greyed, its amount struck through, with "Voided:" and its reason). With no payments: "No payments recorded". A lease with no fees and nothing owed when tracking began shows "No lease fee" and no balance. While a fee is unpaid but still within the waiver days, a line says "Interest accruing: $X, waived if the {month} fee is paid by {date}. Not in the balance yet." While interest is owed, a "Waive Interest" button shows. A "Correct Fee Tracking" button changes the fees-tracked-from date and the amount owed on it. When a late notice is due, the section shows "First Notice due", "Second Notice due" or "Notice of Default due" with "Print Letter" and "Record Notice". Once a notice of default was sent, a box shows where it stands: "Notice of default sent {date} for $X. Cure by {date}: $Y still to pay." (with "Extended from {date}: two-thirds was paid in time." when extended); "Notice of default of {date} cured: $X paid by {date}."; "Notice of default of {date}: $X paid on {date}, after the cure deadline of {date}."; or, in red, "Notice of default of {date} not cured by {date}: $Y of $X still to pay. The trust may end the lease or agree a plan with the homeowner." While the notice is unpaid (cure period running or deadline passed) and the lease has no unresolved foreclosure prevention case, the box has an "Open Prevention Case" button (see [Open Foreclosure Prevention Case from a Lease Default](open-foreclosure-prevention-case-from-default.md)). While the lease has an unresolved case, whichever trigger opened it, the box instead says "Foreclosure prevention case open since {date}" with a "View Case" link that opens the case on the Stewardship page, Foreclosure Prevention tab Payments received on or after the date sent count toward the amount owed; below the payments, the notices sent, newest first (Sent, Level, Delivery, and the balance and oldest unpaid month on that date); then the interest waived, newest first (Waived, Amount, Reason)
11. Panel shows a Mortgages section: Debt Cap ("90% of the purchase option price"), then the permitted mortgages, current first: Lender, Original Principal, Permitted On and how ("Lease signing", "Request" or "Recorded"), and the Notice Address, or "Notice address not on file" in amber. Each current mortgage has "Edit" and "Paid Off"; a paid-off one is greyed with "Paid off {date}". With none: "No permitted mortgages recorded". Buttons: "Record Request" (Active leases only) and "Record Mortgage". Below, Requests newest first (Received, Purpose, Lender, Principal, Status); clicking one opens it
12. User closes the panel
