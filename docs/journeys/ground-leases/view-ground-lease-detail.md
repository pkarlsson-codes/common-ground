# View Ground Lease Detail

1. User navigates to Ground Leases
2. User optionally searches by homeowner name or email (case-insensitive) and filters by status (All Statuses, Active, Inactive); the table lists newest leases first
3. User clicks a row in the Ground Leases table
4. Detail panel opens titled with the homeowner's name (the buyer, for a lease created from a purchase closing; the homeowner entered, for one created from Create Lease), showing status (Active or Inactive), resale formula, and term (years)
5. Depending on the formula, the panel shows Appreciation Share, Index Type, Baseline Index, and/or Annual %
6. Panel shows Base Price and Original Appraised Value (the figures the resale formula starts from; "—" if the lease has none), Improvements Cap if one is set, Start Date, Term, Option Window (days), homeowner Email and Phone (if present), and Covenant Terms (if present)
7. Panel shows Recorded On (the day the lease was recorded in CommonGround, which for a lease that predates CommonGround is later than its Start Date), and a Connected Records section linking to the home (by its label), its land parcel, and, for leases created from a purchase closing, the purchase and the household
8. Panel shows a Resale section: for an Active lease, the resale in progress (notice date, how it came in, Option Window End and days left, or "Window passed", its step, and an "Open Resale" button) or "No resale in progress". For an Inactive lease that ended in a buyback, the section links to the closed resale
9. Panel shows a Lease Fees section: Monthly Fee with its Land Use Fee and Repair Reserve Fee split, Balance as of today ("$120.00 due", in red, "$40.00 credit" or "Paid up"), Oldest Unpaid month and Days Overdue when there is a balance due, a "Record Payment" button, and the payment history newest first (Received, Amount, Method, Reference, and a Void action; a voided payment is greyed, its amount struck through, with "Voided:" and its reason). With no payments: "No payments recorded". A lease with no fees shows "No lease fee" and no balance. When a late notice is due, the section shows "First notice due" or "Second notice due" with "Print Letter" and "Record Notice"; below the payments, the notices sent, newest first (Sent, Level, Delivery, and the balance and oldest unpaid month on that date)
10. User closes the panel
