# Record Lease Fee Notice

For one lease: print its late notice and record that it was sent.

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)), from Ground Leases, the Lease Fees tab or a Lease fee notice item on Today
2. When a notice is due, the Lease Fees section shows "First notice due" or "Second notice due" with "Print Letter" and "Record Notice"
3. User clicks "Print Letter"; the letter opens in a new tab with the print dialog (the same letter as in [Send Lease Fee Notices](../stewardship/send-lease-fee-notices.md))
4. User clicks "Record Notice". "Record Notice" panel opens; its subtitle is the homeowner's name and the balance
5. User picks Level (First Notice or Second Notice, default the one due), Date Sent (default today, not in the future, not before the lease start) and Delivery (default First-Class Mail)
6. User clicks "Record Notice", or "Cancel"
7. Toast: "Notice recorded". The notice appears at the top of the section's notice history with its level, date sent, delivery, and the balance and oldest unpaid month on that date. The "notice due" line goes away until the next notice falls due
8. Recording is refused when the lease owed nothing on the date sent: "This lease had no balance due on that date."

A notice can be recorded when none is due, for example to log a letter sent early.
