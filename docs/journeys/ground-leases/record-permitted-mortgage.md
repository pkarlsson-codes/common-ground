# Record Permitted Mortgage

For a mortgage the trust already permitted that isn't on the lease yet: a lease created with Create Lease or the portfolio import, or a loan permitted before the trust used CommonGround (Art 8.1). A lease from a purchase closing already lists the purchase loan.

1. User opens a ground lease (see [View Ground Lease Detail](view-ground-lease-detail.md)) and clicks "Record Mortgage" in the Mortgages section
2. "Record Permitted Mortgage" panel opens; its subtitle is the homeowner's name
3. User enters Lender or Servicer (required), Notice Address (optional, several lines: where the trust sends copies of notices of default), Original Principal ($, required, above zero) and Permitted On (required, not in the future, not before the lease start)
4. User clicks "Record Mortgage", or "Cancel"
5. Toast: "Mortgage recorded". The mortgage appears in the Mortgages section, permitted "Recorded"
