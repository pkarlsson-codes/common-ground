# Record Improvement Completed

For when the homeowner has finished approved work and sent the receipts. Staff set the credit that counts toward the resale price.

1. In the Improvement Request panel (see [View Improvement Request](view-improvement-request.md)) of an approved request, user clicks "Record Completed"
2. "Record Improvement Completed" panel opens showing the description, Estimated Cost and Pre-Approved Credit
3. User enters Completed On (default today, not in the future, not before the approval date) and Actual Cost ($, required, above zero)
4. User adds the receipts (choose or drop; PDF or image; at least one required)
5. User enters Confirmed Credit ($, default the lesser of Actual Cost and Pre-Approved Credit; zero up to Pre-Approved Credit) and optional Notes. The panel says "The credit can't be more than the $X agreed before the work."
6. User clicks "Record Completed", or "Cancel"
7. Toast: "Improvement recorded". The request shows Completed. The lease's Improvements section shows the new total of Confirmed Improvements and, when a cap applies, "Counted at resale: $X of $Y cap"
8. If a receipt upload fails, the panel stays open with the error and nothing is recorded. If the uploads work but recording fails, trying again reuses the uploaded files
