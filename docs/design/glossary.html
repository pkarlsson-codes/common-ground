Glossary

The language of each bounded context: its nouns, the verbs that act on each noun, what each one means inside that context, and the words it does not use. A term means what its own row says; the same word elsewhere can mean something else. Docs, journeys, UI labels and code use these terms. Source of the words: the Grounded Solutions CLT Technical Manual (2011), read as language, not as a model to copy. Why each term was chosen: Glossary Decisions. How a party's name changes between contexts: Architecture, Messaging between contexts.

Last checked against the code: 2026-11-14.

How to read this page

Each context has one table. A noun row is followed by the verbs that act on that noun, indented. A verb means what its own row says, for that noun only: "withdraw" under Application and "withdraw" under Purchase workflow are two verbs that are spelled the same. Each verb row names the event the code raises for it.

5. Home Buyers

TermMeaningDo not use
HouseholdThe people who would live in a home, with their income, size and circumstances. It exists before any application and after any purchase.family, client, customer, buyer, homebuyer
registerStaff add a household, before or without an application. Event: HouseholdRegistered.create, sign up
update circumstancesStaff change the household's income or other facts because they changed. Event: HouseholdCircumstancesUpdated. See Not yet matching.
Household memberOne person in a household.member (alone; Identity has trust members), occupant
add / update / removeStaff add a person to the household, change their details, or take them off it. Events: HouseholdMemberAdded, HouseholdMemberUpdated, HouseholdMemberRemoved.delete
ReadinessWhether a household has completed its three readiness items: housing counseling, CLT orientation and mortgage prequalification (a lender's estimate of what the household can borrow, valid until an expiry date). Readiness is complete when all three are on record; an expired prequalification still counts. To be selected, a household needs complete readiness and a prequalification still valid on the selection date. The UI shows a household as Ready only when its prequalification also hasn't expired. Code: ReadinessItem, IsReadinessComplete, IsPrequalificationValidOn.buyer preparation ("buyer" is Homeownership's word), onboarding; "prequalification" alone is fine, "mortgage" alone is not
recordStaff enter that the household completed housing counseling or CLT orientation, or got a prequalification, outside the app. Events: HousingCounselingRecorded, CltOrientationRecorded, MortgagePrequalificationRecorded.complete, check off
expireA prequalification reaches its expiry date and no longer counts toward selection. Event: MortgagePrequalificationExpired.lapse
ApplicationA household's request to buy a home from the trust, with a snapshot of the household's facts at the time. Code name today: HomeownerApplication.request, submission
submitThe household applies. Refused while the household has another open application. Event: ApplicationSubmitted.create, file
approve / denyThe trust puts the application on the waiting list, or refuses it with a reason. The approval date sets its place on the waiting list. Events: ApplicationApproved, ApplicationDenied.accept, reject
remove from the waiting listThe trust takes an approved application off the waiting list, for a reason that arose after approval. Event: ApplicationRemovedFromWaitingList.deny (that is before approval)
withdrawThe household stops its application. Event: ApplicationWithdrawn.cancel
return to the waiting listThe purchase for the home it was selected for fell through, so the application waits again with its original approval date. Happens on its own. Event: ApplicationReturnedToWaitingList.reinstate, reapprove
ApplicantA household in the role of having an application. Only when the sentence is about the application ("the applicant was selected"). Otherwise say household. No verbs of its own.buyer
Waiting listThe approved applications waiting for a home, in approval-date order. No verbs of its own: applications join and leave it through their own verbs.wait list, queue, pool
Home selectionThe trust choosing one household for one home from the waiting list as it is, in one step, recorded once with every application on the list and whether it was chosen, passed over or couldn't be chosen (with the reasons). Short form in running text: "selection"; in the app, "chosen". A lottery draws the household at random from the lottery pool, the households it may draw.assignment, matching, allocation
select by priorityStaff choose a household for a home, citing the priorities that put it ahead of the others. Each household's income is judged against the home's income limit for its household size on the date of the choice. The application leaves the waiting list and a purchase begins in Homeownership. Events: HouseholdSelectedByPriority, ApplicationSelectedForHome.assign, match, award
draw the lotteryStaff have the app choose a household at random, from the households found tied or from the whole waiting list, leaving out any that can't be chosen. Events: HouseholdSelectedByLottery, ApplicationSelectedForHome.run, roll
pass overAn application that could have been chosen when another household was. It stays on the waiting list, and the passing over is on its record. An application that couldn't have been chosen isn't passed over. Event: ApplicationPassedOver.skip, reject
Income limit scheduleThe trust's table of income limits (maximum household income by household size), effective from a date.AMI cap, eligibility threshold; "schedule" alone (Homeownership has notice schedules)
enterStaff add the limits that take effect on a date. Event: IncomeLimitScheduleEntered.create, upload
correctStaff fix a schedule that was entered wrong. Event: IncomeLimitScheduleCorrected.edit, update

6. Homeownership

TermMeaningDo not use
Purchase workflowThe steps from a selected buyer to closing. UI and journeys say "purchase". Code: PurchaseWorkflow.sale, transaction, deal
initiateStart a purchase. Happens on its own when a household is selected; staff initiate a direct purchase. Event: PurchaseWorkflowInitiated.start, open
record (a step)Staff enter that a step of the purchase happened outside the app: appraisal completed, inspection completed, title cleared, financing committed, disclosure acknowledged, lease executed, mortgage loan closed. Events: AppraisalCompleted, InspectionCompleted, TitleCleared, FinancingCommitted, BuyerDisclosureAcknowledged, LeaseExecuted. Buyer eligibility is also recorded today; see Not yet matching.complete, check off
correct (a step)Staff fix the figures of a recorded eligibility, appraisal or financing step that were wrong, with a reason. The old figures stay in the purchase's history. Only while the purchase is open. Events: BuyerEligibilityCorrected, AppraisalCorrected, FinancingCorrected.edit, update, amend
schedule the closingStaff set the closing date. Event: ClosingScheduled.book
stallMark the purchase as stuck, with a reason and a date it should be resolved by. Event: WorkflowStalled.pause, block
closeThe purchase completes: the money changed hands. The buyer becomes the homeowner and a ground lease is created. Event: PurchaseClosed.complete, finish
failThe purchase ends because a condition wasn't met, such as financing falling through. Needs a failure reason. Event: PurchaseFailed.reject, cancel
withdrawThe buyer drops out of the purchase. Event: PurchaseWithdrawn.cancel, abandon
BuyerThe party on a purchase workflow, from selection until closing. Usually a household selected in Home Buyers; on a direct purchase, the person staff entered. No verbs of its own.purchaser, homebuyer, household (Homeownership has no households)
HomeownerThe person or people who own a home on trust land and hold its ground lease. A buyer becomes a homeowner when the purchase closes. No verbs of its own.lessee, leaseholder, owner, resident
Ground lease templateThe trust's standard lease terms that new ground leases are made from.lease template, model lease (that is the manual's document)
createStaff define a template's name, covenant terms and option window. Event: GroundLeaseTemplateCreated.define, add
archive / restoreStaff hide a template from new leases, or bring it back. Events: GroundLeaseTemplateArchived, GroundLeaseTemplateRestored.delete, retire
Ground leaseThe long lease from the trust to the homeowner for the land under the home.land lease, lease (alone)
createHappens on its own when a purchase closes. Staff create one for a lease signed before the trust used the app; refused while the home is leased or has a purchase in progress (as it does once a household is chosen). Events: GroundLeaseCreatedFromPurchase, GroundLeaseCreated.register, establish
terminateStaff end the lease, with a reason and a date. Event: GroundLeaseTerminated.close, cancel
record a stewardship check-inStaff enter a visit with the homeowner and its notes. Event: StewardshipCheckInRecorded.log
record a resale price statementStaff enter that the trust sent the homeowner what the home may sell for under the lease's formula, with the figures used. Event: ResalePriceStatementRecorded.
Lease feeThe monthly fee the homeowner pays the trust under the ground lease.ground rent, rent, ground lease fee
correct trackingStaff fix the day fees are tracked from and the opening balance, such as after an import with the wrong date. Event: LeaseFeeTrackingCorrected.edit, reset
waive interestStaff forgive late-fee interest owed today, with a reason, such as for a household in hardship. At most the interest owed today. Event: LeaseFeeInterestWaived.forgive, cancel, write off
Lease fee paymentMoney the trust received toward a lease's fees.transaction, charge
recordStaff enter a payment the trust received. An ended lease still takes payments. Event: LeaseFeePaymentRecorded.post, add
voidStaff mark a payment recorded by mistake so it no longer counts. It stays on the lease, marked void. Needs a reason. Event: LeaseFeePaymentVoided.delete, reverse, refund
Payoff statementWhat a lease owes in fees and interest through a chosen date, printed for the closing agent when the home is resold. Each print is kept on the resale.payoff letter, demand
recordStaff print a payoff statement. Event: PayoffStatementRecorded.generate, issue
Fees paid from proceedsThe part of the seller's sale proceeds the trust receives toward unpaid lease fees at closing. Recorded as a lease fee payment with method "Sale proceeds".deduction, holdback
Lease fee noticeA late notice the trust sent, keeping the balance and oldest unpaid month on the day it was sent. Each has a level: first, second or default. The default one is the notice of default, which gives a cure period. Code: LeaseFeeNotice, LeaseFeeNoticeLevel."notice" alone (the trust sends other notices), bill, invoice
recordStaff enter that a notice was sent. Refused when nothing was owed that day. Event: LeaseFeeNoticeRecorded.send, issue (the app doesn't send it)
Lease fee notice policyThe trust's rule for when lease fee notices go out."policy" alone
setStaff replace the current policy with a new one. Event: LeaseFeeNoticePolicySet.configure, edit
Permitted mortgageA mortgage the trust has allowed on the home.leasehold mortgage, loan, mortgage (alone; Home Buyers has mortgage prequalification)
recordStaff enter a mortgage the trust permitted outside the app, such as on a lease from before the trust used it. Event: PermittedMortgageRecorded.add
editStaff change a mortgage's details because they changed, such as servicing moving to another company. Event: PermittedMortgageEdited.update, correct
mark paid offStaff enter that the loan was repaid, so its lender no longer gets copies of notices. Event: PermittedMortgagePaidOff.close, remove
Mortgage requestA homeowner asking the trust to permit a new loan on the home.loan application, mortgage application
receiveStaff enter the homeowner's request. Refused unless the lease is active, or while another request on the lease is pending. Event: MortgageRequestReceived.submit (the homeowner's act, not entered by staff)
record the appraisalStaff enter the loan's appraisal and closing costs; the app works out the debt check against the purchase option price. Event: MortgageRequestAppraisalRecorded.
approve / denyThe trust gives its written permission for the loan, or refuses it with the reason its denial letter gives. Approving needs the debt check; a loan above the cap needs a reason. Events: MortgageRequestApproved, MortgageRequestDenied.accept, reject
record the loan closedStaff enter that the approved loan closed. It becomes a permitted mortgage, and the mortgages it paid off are marked paid off. Event: MortgageRequestLoanClosed.
withdrawThe homeowner drops the loan, before a decision or after approval but before it closed. Needs a reason. Event: MortgageRequestWithdrawn.cancel
Insurance requirementsThe coverage a ground lease requires.insurance policy
setStaff replace the requirements on a lease or template. Event: InsuranceRequirementsSet.configure
Insurance certificateThe homeowner's proof of insurance cover.policy, proof
recordStaff enter a certificate the trust received. Event: InsuranceCertificateRecorded.upload (the file is a document)
removeStaff take out a certificate recorded in error, so it no longer counts toward the cover. It stays on the lease. Needs a reason. Event: InsuranceCertificateRemoved. See Not yet matching.delete
Improvement requestA homeowner asking the trust to approve altering or adding to the home, and to name the credit it will count at resale. The confirmed credit is what the trust counts once the work is done.alteration request, capital improvement (alone), renovation
receiveStaff enter the homeowner's request. Event: ImprovementRequestReceived.submit (the homeowner's act, not entered by staff)
approve / denyThe trust agrees to the work and names the credit it will count, or refuses with the reason its letter gives. Events: ImprovementRequestApproved, ImprovementRequestDenied.accept, reject
record completedStaff enter that the approved work is finished, with its cost, the receipts and the confirmed credit. Event: ImprovementCompleted.close, finish
Heir transferThe home passing to an heir when the homeowner dies (Art 10.3). The lease continues with the heir as homeowner; the base price and everything on the lease stay. Different from a resale, which ends the lease.inheritance, succession, probate transfer
open / record heirStaff open the transfer from the executor's notice, then enter the heir and the Art 10.3 route (spouse, child, household member, other). Events: HeirTransferOpened, HeirRecorded.register, name
complete / closeComplete: the signed letters are on file and the lease names the heir. Close: the home doesn't pass to the heir. Events: HeirTransferCompleted, HeirTransferClosed.finalize, reject
withdrawThe homeowner drops the work, before a decision or after approval but before it is completed. Needs a reason. Event: ImprovementRequestWithdrawn.cancel
ResaleA homeowner's sale of the home back into the trust's program. It starts with the notice of intent to sell. The trust then has the purchase option, its right to buy the home at the purchase option price, for the lease's option window. A buyback is a resale the trust itself bought.sale, listing, exit; "option" alone; "purchase" for the purchase option
record the intent to sellStaff enter the homeowner's notice; the resale opens and the option window starts on the notice date. Events: IntentToSellRecorded, ResaleStarted. See Not yet matching.
record the appraisalStaff enter the appraisal the lease requires; the app works out the formula price and the purchase option price. A later appraisal replaces it until the trust decides. Event: ResaleAppraisalRecorded.
exercise the purchase optionThe trust decides to buy the home at the purchase option price. Event: PurchaseOptionExercised.buy, accept
decline the purchase optionThe trust decides not to buy. Not built yet.pass, reject
close the buybackStaff enter that the trust paid for and took the home. The seller's lease ends; the home goes to the waiting list or the trust holds it with a reason. Event: BuybackClosed.complete
withdrawThe homeowner takes back the notice of intent to sell. Event: ResaleWithdrawn.cancel
AppraisalAn appraiser's market valuation, recorded for a purchase, a resale or a mortgage request. Its verbs are under each of those.assessment (that is Property Registry's assessed value)

7. Stewardship

TermMeaningDo not use
Stewardship caseThe trust's permanent file for one ground lease. It holds the lease's lease violations, occupancy verifications and property inspections.ticket, incident, file; "case" alone (Stewardship has two kinds)
open / closeHappens on its own: opened when the ground lease starts, closed when it ends. Events: StewardshipCaseOpened, StewardshipCaseClosed.create, archive
Lease violationA breach of the ground lease's terms. A violation notice tells the homeowner.infraction, breach (alone)
identifyStaff note that the ground lease has been breached. Event: LeaseViolationIdentified.report, log, open

cureThe homeowner fixed the breach and staff confirmed it. Event: LeaseViolationCured.resolve, fix, close
escalateThe homeowner didn't cure after the notice; the trust takes it further. Event: LeaseViolationEscalated.
Occupancy verificationThe check that the homeowner lives in the home as the ground lease requires.occupancy (alone; that is Property Registry's Vacant/Occupied)
initiateStaff start the check, with a due date. Event: OccupancyVerificationInitiated.start, request
submitThe homeowner's response is entered. Event: OccupancyVerificationSubmitted.
verifyStaff confirm the homeowner lives there. Event: OccupancyVerified.approve, accept
failStaff find the homeowner doesn't meet the requirement, with a reason. Event: OccupancyVerificationFailed.reject (see Not yet matching)
mark overdueNo response came by the due date. Event: OccupancyVerificationOverdue.expire
Property inspectionA visit to check the home's condition against the ground lease.inspection (alone; Homeownership has the purchase's inspection), visit
scheduleStaff set a date for it. Event: PropertyInspectionScheduled.book
completeStaff enter that it happened and what was found. Event: PropertyInspectionCompleted.record, close
cancelStaff call it off. Event: PropertyInspectionCancelled.withdraw
Foreclosure prevention caseThe trust's work with a homeowner who is behind on a permitted mortgage or on lease fees. A lease has at most one unresolved case.default case
openStaff open a case. Opening for a lease default notice (an unpaid notice of default, with its cure-by date) keeps the notice's figures. Event: ForeclosurePreventionCaseOpened.create
record servicer delinquencyStaff enter what the loan servicer reported about missed payments. Event: ServicerDelinquencyRecorded.
make a counseling referral / record the counseling outcomeStaff refer the homeowner to foreclosure counseling, then enter how it went. Events: CounselingReferralMade, CounselingOutcomeRecorded.
request forbearance / record the forbearance outcomeThe trust asks the lender to hold off, then enters the lender's answer. Event: ForbearanceOutcomeRecorded.
activate the cure periodThe trust uses its right under the lender recognition agreement to cure the default itself. Event: CurePeriodActivated.
resolveStaff end the case with an outcome, which may not be a cure. Event: ForeclosurePreventionCaseResolved.close, cure

9. Reminders

Work that has no record behind it, which staff set for a day so it shows on Today. A reminder reads nothing from other contexts.

TermMeaningDo not use
ReminderA dated note set for the trust so the work shows on Today. It belongs to the trust: every member sees it, whatever their focus. It is not a task: it has no owner, status or priority. Code: Reminder.task, to-do, follow-up, alert
NoteWhat to do, in staff's own words. At most 500 characters.description, message
Date (remind on)The day the reminder starts showing on Today. Not a deadline: a reminder is never due or overdue. Code: RemindOn.due date, deadline
SectionThe Today section a reminder shows in: Applicants, Selections, Purchases, Stewardship or Records. A reminder set in none shows in the Reminders section.area (for a reminder), category
Upcoming / showing / doneWhere a reminder stands: upcoming before its date, showing on Today from its date until someone marks it done, then done.pending, active, open, closed
setStaff set a reminder for a date, with a note and optionally a section. Event: ReminderSet.create, add, schedule
mark doneAny member of the trust marks a reminder done, and it leaves Today. Event: ReminderMarkedDone.complete, close, dismiss, resolve

11. Not yet matching

  • Code HomeownerApplication vs "application". Not renamed yet: it appears in hundreds of identifiers (HomeownerApplicationId alone about 87) and in event names. The name misleads because the household is not yet a homeowner.
  • Code UnitAssessment (in HomeAssessment.cs) vs "home assessment". It also carries an AppraiserName, which blurs assessment and appraisal.
  • Occupancy verification: UI "Reject", code "fail". stewardship/reject-occupancy-verification.md has a "Reject" button; the code is FailOccupancy and OccupancyVerificationFailed, and the toast says "Verification failed".
  • Land parcel "withdraw for redevelopment" (LandParcel.WithdrawForRedevelopment) is the trust taking its own parcel out. The naming convention keeps "withdraw" for a party stopping something it started.
  • Remove an insurance certificate (RemoveInsuranceCertificate) undoes an entry made in error, which the naming convention calls void, as VoidLeaseFeePayment does.
  • Readiness: UI heading "Buyer Preparation Checklist" (HouseholdDetailPanel.tsx). The term is readiness; "buyer" is Homeownership's word.
  • Submit an application: UI "Start Application", toast "Application started", on the household detail. The verb is submit (ApplicationSubmitted).
  • Household member: UI says "member" alone ("Add Member", "Save Member", toasts "Member added", "Member updated", "Member removed"). Inside the household panel the meaning is clear, but the term is household member.
  • Edit vs update. EditPermittedMortgage and UpdateHouseholdCircumstances / UpdateHouseholdMember mean the same thing.
  • Record as a catch-all. Homeownership has 18 Record* commands. Some hide a trust decision under "record", for example RecordEligibilityVerified (staff verify) and RecordIntentToSell (staff receive a notice).
  • Stewardship code says "lease" alone (LeaseViolation, LeaseDefaultNotice, LeaseFeeArrearsRecord) for the ground lease. Accepted inside compound names; docs say "ground lease" when the lease itself is meant.
  • Journeys say "user" for staff about 500 times. Most are UI actions and fine. Only sentences that say what staff decide need changing.
  • Team journeys: team/remove-a-member.md says "member" alone; the term is trust member (staff).
  • Resale journeys name a "Decline to Buy" button and a "Decline Purchase Option" journey. The verb is "decline the purchase option".
  • The lease fee notice policy is Homeownership's, but its journey sits in docs/journeys/trust-policy/, and the nav says Trust Management holds "policy".
