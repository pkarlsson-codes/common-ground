Property Registry

The trust's land and the homes on it: what the trust owns, what each home is, what it was assessed at, who may buy it, and where it stands in being sold.

Projects: src/CommonGround.PropertyRegistry, src/CommonGround.PropertyRegistry.Contracts. Last checked against the code: 2026-11-13. Read the Architecture Overview and the Shared Kernel page first.

1. What it is

The CLT idea behind it. A community land trust buys land and keeps it for good. Homes on that land are sold to households with modest incomes. The household owns the house; the trust keeps the land and leases it to the household under a ground lease. The lease caps the price when the house is resold, so it stays affordable for the next buyer. Before any of that can happen, the trust needs a record of its land and its homes. That record is this context.

What it owns:

  • Land parcels: the land the trust holds, with its legal parcel number, address, size, how and when the trust acquired it, and any covenant terms. A parcel can be decommissioned and recommissioned. It is never sold.
  • Homes: each home on a parcel, with its type, bedrooms, bathrooms, size, year built, accessibility, assessment history, and income limit (the most a buying household may earn, as a percent of area median income).
  • Development projects: new construction on a parcel, or rehabilitation of homes already on it.

2. The model

ID prefixes: parcel-, home-, project-.

LandParcel

  • A parcel is never sold. It can be decommissioned and recommissioned. Repeating either does nothing.
  • A decommissioned parcel is hidden from the parcel list unless the caller asks for it (IncludeDecommissioned).

Home

  • The label a person reads, "14 Alder Lane, Unit B", is the parcel address plus UnitDesignation. HomeLabel.Format builds it when read or published. It is never stored on the home.
  • IncomeLimitPercentOfAmi is the most a buying household may earn, as a percent of area median income: 1 to 200, or null for no limit (a market-rate home). Home.Register applies no default. The web form starts at 80.
  • An assessment can't be negative or dated after today in the trust's time zone. Every assessment is kept, as a plain decimal, not Money.

DevelopmentProject

  • New construction on a parcel, or rehabilitation of named homes on it. It has one event, so its status is always Initiated.

  • HomeRegistered carries the home's label, bedrooms, accessibility and income limit. HomeBuyers keeps them to compare the waiting list against the home. Homeownership keeps the label to name the home on purchases.
  • HomeIncomeLimitSet updates HomeBuyers' copy of the limit.
  • HomeSaleStageChanged tells HomeBuyers where the home now stands, with the date it got there, a sequence number so late news is ignored, and the latest selection it accounts for. HomeBuyers chooses a household only for an Offered home. It is published only when one of those changes, and it is the only use of the stage outside this context; keep it that narrow.
  • The domain and integration events share these two names. The handlers alias them DomainHomeRegistered and IntegrationHomeRegistered.
  • Parcel and project events stay inside this context.
  • Every event it consumes updates home availability, below.

4. Home availability

Staff need to see where each home stands in being sold. Those facts belong to HomeBuyers and Homeownership, so this context only listens. HomeAvailabilityView collects their events per home and works out a SaleStage. NotForSale means a homeowner leases the home (Occupied). Every other stage is Vacant.

  Offered --> UnderContract : ApplicationSelected
  UnderContract --> Offered : PurchaseFellThrough
  UnderContract --> NotForSale : GroundLeaseCreated
  Offered --> NotForSale : GroundLeaseCreated (direct)
  NotForSale --> AwaitingTrustDecision : ResaleOpened
  AwaitingTrustDecision --> NotForSale : ResaleWithdrawn
  AwaitingTrustDecision --> Offered : ResaleBoughtBack, offered
  AwaitingTrustDecision --> HeldByTrust : ResaleBoughtBack, held
  HeldByTrust --> Offered : release-for-sale
  • No view means Offered. A home has no view until the first event about it arrives.
  • It never refuses an event. The other contexts already decided. Events can arrive in any order, so the view stores everything it has heard and Refresh() works the stage out again each time. The selection recorded last counts, ordered by when it was recorded, since staff can date a choice earlier.
  • HomeBuyers refuses on it. Every change to the stage is published as HomeSaleStageChanged, from HomeAvailabilityUpdate.Record, and HomeBuyers won't choose a household for a home that isn't Offered. So the stage decides something after all, in HomeBuyers.
  • A buyback starts over. Leases and selections before the buyback date stop counting.
  • Release for sale is the one change staff make here. POST /api/homes/{id}/release-for-sale moves a home the trust held after a buyback to Offered. It raises no event.
  • Today shows every Offered home as "Available" or "Available again after …" (a purchase fell through, a buyback, or a release).

Code: Application/ReadModels/HomeAvailabilityView.cs, updated only through Application/EventHandlers/HomeAvailabilityUpdate.cs.

6. Gaps

  • RegisterHome doesn't check that the parcel exists or is active. On a missing parcel, HomeRegistered carries a label with an empty address.
  • DecommissionParcel doesn't check for homes or open ground leases on the parcel.
  • Development projects don't check the parcel, or that a rehabilitation's homes are on it.
  • POST /api/development-projects returns the list URL as its location, not the new project's.
  • No callers outside unit tests: ListParcelForPlacement, LandParcel.ListForPlacement and WithdrawForRedevelopment (their events have no Apply), the three construction events, ProjectStatus values other than Initiated, the pg_trgm extension, and AddPropertyRegistry().

7. Changing this context

  • A fact other contexts need: add it to the integration event in PropertyRegistry.Contracts and update the HomeBuyers and Homeownership consumers in the same change.
  • A new event that moves a home's stage: handle it through HomeAvailabilityUpdate, add a Record… method to HomeAvailabilityView, and test it arriving in any order in HomeAvailabilityViewTests.
  • A new list filter: filter in the query, not after paging. GetHomes shows how, including homes with no availability view yet.
